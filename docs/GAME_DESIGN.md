# Project Nova – Game Design

## Vision

Project Nova is an original sci-fi FPS inspired by classic immersive
science-fiction shooters.

The goal is to combine exploration, combat, physics, puzzles and
storytelling with significantly more detailed environments.

## Setting

The game takes place inside the Nova Research Facility.

The facility contains:

- Research laboratories
- Engineering areas
- Security zones
- Power systems
- Underground sections
- Restricted research areas

## Gameplay

The player can:

- Walk, sprint, crouch and jump
- Use weapons
- Interact with objects
- Activate computers and terminals
- Solve environmental puzzles
- Manipulate physics objects
- Explore the facility
- Fight enemies

## Weapons

### Nova Sidearm
A compact energy weapon.

### Kinetic Carbine
A medium-range weapon designed for combat against stronger enemies.

### Arc Tool
A utility device used for interacting with electrical systems
and solving environmental puzzles.

## Enemies

### Crawler
A fast hostile creature that moves through the facility.

### Sentinel
An automated security drone.

### Warden
A larger armored enemy encountered later in the game.

## Story

### Chapter 1 – Incident Zero

The player arrives at the Nova Research Facility after a major
communication failure.

The facility appears abandoned.

Something went wrong.

The player must restore power and find out what happened.

## Visual Direction

Project Nova should feature:

- Detailed environments
- High-resolution textures
- Dynamic lighting
- HDR lighting
- Particle effects
- Atmospheric effects
- Detailed machinery
- Destructible and interactive objects

## Development Rule

All original characters, maps, weapons, textures, sounds and story
elements will be created for Project Nova.

No copyrighted Half-Life assets will be copied.
